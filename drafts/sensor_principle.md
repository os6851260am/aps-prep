## 1. Temp. Sensor

### Sensor

There’re 3 kinds of temp. sensor: RTD, Thermocouple and Thermistor. We used the NTC Thermistor because of its high sensitivity and low cost.

### Principle

NTC means Negative Temp. Coefficient so the resistance would decrease as the temp. increase.

### Conditioning Circuit

The R (resistance) is not measurable to ADC/MCU, so we need to transfer it to standard electronic signals. We adopted the serial resistor voltage divider and parallel resistor to make it linear.
